fzwift

An indoor cycling game for your smart trainer. Ride a low-poly island with AI riders, real drafting and a switchback summit climb, then export your ride as a .fit file.

Opening it the first time

This is an early preview and isn't signed by Apple yet, so macOS will block it the first time. To open it:

  1. Open the .dmg and drag fzwift into Applications.
  2. Open fzwift once. macOS says it can't verify the app; click Done.
  3. Go to System Settings → Privacy & Security, scroll down, and click Open Anyway next to fzwift. Confirm with your password.

Or, in Terminal: xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine /Applications/fzwift.app

The first time you pair sensors, macOS asks for Bluetooth access. Click Allow.

What's in it

  • Smart trainers over Bluetooth: any FTMS trainer (Wahoo KICKR, Tacx, Elite, Zwift Hub and more) sets resistance from the road grade. Adjustable trainer difficulty.
  • Sensors: Bluetooth power meters, heart rate straps and cadence sensors. No sensors? Ride with the keyboard.
  • An island to ride: about 15 km of roads: a coastal loop, rolling hills and a 1.9 km summit climb, with junctions where you choose your route.
  • Riders and drafting: AI riders to chase and draft, with a nearby-riders list and minimap.
  • Your data: save rides as .fit files to upload to Strava, Garmin Connect or TrainingPeaks. Runs offline; no account needed.
